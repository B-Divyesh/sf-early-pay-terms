How it works

Create payment terms in three steps

  1. Enter invoice figuresAdd the net amount, tax, reference, and dates.

What this calculator does not do

Tax and discount rules vary by contract and region. This calculator applies your choices. It does not decide what is legal or create accounting entries. Check the payment card before sending it.
