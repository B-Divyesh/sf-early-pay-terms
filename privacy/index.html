Policy / 28 August 2026

Privacy, without small-print surprises.

Your invoice figures are kept locally in your browser. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party scripts.

Data on this device

The current draft, saved versions, and templates are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. Your license token and last verification result are stored in localStorage. This data stays on this device unless you export it or clear the site’s storage.

License verification and checkout

When you restore or use Plus, the license token is sent to Sociobot’s billing API only to check whether it is valid. Checkout opens a Sociobot-hosted page; Sociobot and its payment processor handle the purchase details under their own notices. Early Pay Terms never receives your card number.

Exports and deletion

JSON and CSV exports are created on your device and downloaded through your browser. JSON does not include the license token. Use “Clear local data” in the calculator to erase invoice drafts, versions, and templates. Browser site controls can also remove the license.

Network and offline use

A service worker caches the application files so the calculator can work offline. No invoice content is sent when the app updates its cache. We do not collect a page-view count in version 1.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in. This policy may change if the product’s data handling changes; the effective date above will be updated.
